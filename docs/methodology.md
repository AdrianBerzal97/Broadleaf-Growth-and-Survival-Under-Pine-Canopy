# Study methodology — documented scope

This project documents an undergraduate study of broadleaf planting beneath a *Pinus pinaster* plantation. The original thesis investigated thinning treatments (including a no-thinning control and treatments at 20% and 35% basal area) and their relationship to understory light, survival, growth, and competing vegetation.

The four study species were *Sorbus torminalis*, *Quercus ilex*, *Quercus pyrenaica*, and *Acer monspessulanum*. The thesis describes evaluation five years after planting.

The original thesis should be consulted for the precise experimental design, definitions of thinning codes, sampling units, field measurement protocols, statistical model specifications, and interpretation of results. These details are **not reconstructed from assumptions** in this repository.

## Evidence distinction

Findings summarized in `README.md` are taken from the thesis abstract. They are not new analyses, independently replicated estimates, or outputs generated by the original scripts.
