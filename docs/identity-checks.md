# Cross-sheet identity checks

Read-only workbook comparison found 401 matched provisional plant identities between the combined and 2023 worksheets, including alphanumeric identifiers 4A and 4B. Among matched height/diameter pairs, 325 were identical, 74 differed by missing-value notation, and two had numerical conflicts requiring field-sheet verification. These findings are diagnostic, not corrections to the original observations.

The longitudinal survival worksheet has 353 records; 351 provisional identifiers overlap with the 2023 sheet, with 134 conflicting binary survival values. The two sources should not be treated as interchangeable. Exact input selection and denominator definitions remain unresolved.

No raw workbook, plant-level data table, or modified original R script is included.
