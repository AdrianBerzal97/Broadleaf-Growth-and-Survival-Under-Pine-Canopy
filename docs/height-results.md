# Mean seedling height by thinning treatment

![Mean height by thinning treatment](../figures/mean_height_by_treatment_2018_2023.svg)

| Treatment | 2018 (cm) | 2019 (cm) | 2023 (cm) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CB-20 | 49.47 | 55.67 | 116.37 |
| CB-35 | 47.75 | 54.62 | 73.65 |
| Control | 46.21 | 54.26 | 85.72 |
| SL-35 | 47.02 | 54.48 | 63.44 |

**Source:** Original undergraduate thesis, Appendix Table A.18, *Altura promedio de los individuos plantados por tratamiento en los tres años de medición*. Original decimal commas have been transcribed as decimal points. The thesis methods specify direct plant-height measurements in centimetres; the main-text figure captions refer to metres, so this visualization uses **centimetres** to match the recorded magnitude and measurement description.

These are **reported mean heights**, not annual growth rates. Measurement years are unequally spaced (one year between 2018 and 2019; four years between 2019 and 2023), so connecting lines are descriptive and should not be interpreted as equal-interval trends. No error bars, significance markers, or recalculated statistics are implied.

The thesis main-text discussion describes the relative treatment ordering somewhat differently from the Appendix A.18 means. This visualization uses the tabulated numbers directly, without silently reconciling that discrepancy.

Raw measurements remain unpublished, and the means have not been independently recalculated from the original spreadsheet.
